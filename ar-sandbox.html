How to use this

On your iPhone or Android: tap the AR icon that appears in the bottom-right of the viewer. Your phone will open its native AR viewer (Quick Look on iOS, Scene Viewer on Android), you'll see a reticle on the ground, tap to place, then walk around the object.

To test your own model:

  • Upload a .glb file somewhere public — Supabase storage works, or a GitHub repo raw URL
  • Make sure the host serves it with Access-Control-Allow-Origin: * — otherwise CORS will block it
  • Paste the URL into the GLB field above and hit Load
  • For iOS AR, also provide a .usdz version — convert from .glb using Apple's Reality Converter or an online tool

Sourcing models: Poly Haven (CC0), Kenney assets (CC0), Sketchfab (check the license filter — avoid CC-BY-NC for a commercial project), or generate with Meshy / Luma.

Keep them small. Under 5MB is the sweet spot. Bigger models take forever to load on cell networks and drain battery fast in AR.
